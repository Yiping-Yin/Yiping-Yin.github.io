Method & evaluation details

Within a pair, one leg leads the other. The position trades the reversal of their spread rather than either leg’s direction, cancelling the panel’s common factor (21.6% of its correlation) inside the pair. Directional accuracy was 54.07%, distributed across about thirty of fifty-one names.

The General-round score was 984.62; leaderboard position was recorded as of 3 August 2026. The final technical result was 0.4 of one block’s standard error from the public-data replay mean, 3.7 points behind second place. The public research record documents the method and its evidence limits.

Order-book analytics & ETF pricingQuantitative research · Feb – May 2025

Reconstructing limit-order books and modelling ETF fair value, with strict timestamp alignment and explicit assumptions in historical market-making diagnostics.

L1–L8 order booksETF fair valueHistorical diagnostics

Data, models & historical diagnostics

Reconciled 4,406 PXA trades to 24,303 eligible L1–L8 order-book states using same-venue, strict-prior matching. Benchmarked four mid-price estimators and produced 34,044 rolling-100 diagnostics with zero look-ahead rows.

A five-second NDQ fair-value/iNAV model used 4,319 synchronized ETF, NQ futures and AUD/USD observations. The beta-1 specification recorded 4.30 bps MAE and 0.999 correlation on 4,259 post-calibration rows.

A 64-session chronological minute-bar-proxy market-making study included a 12-session historical diagnostic partition. Under identical proxy assumptions, inventory and imbalance controls reduced fills from 1,863 to 1,014 and maximum inventory from 500 to 336; diagnostic net P&L rose from 56.53 to 147.21.

Cross-asset market making & executionOptiver × UNSW · May – Aug 2026

A market-making system connecting an equity basket, futures, ETF and options through common index units, depth-matched execution and inventory-aware hedging.

Market microstructureBlack-76 optionsDelta hedging

Microstructure study & simulation results

Analyzed 1,003 timestamp-matched OB5X futures–ETF order-book observations from a 20-minute Optibook session. Lead-lag was negligible within three seconds; return correlation rose from 0.032 at one second to 0.522 at 120 seconds, consistent with an Epps-effect pattern.

Normalized the five-stock basket, three futures and ETF into common index units. The system executed depth-matched ETF–futures IOC pairs and quoted two Black-76 options with inventory-aware skew and threshold-based delta hedging.

A one-hour Optibook simulation produced +36.50 P&L change on 1,222 lots and 187 fills. Of these, 39 arbitrage pairs matched 892 lots. All 6,972 quote cycles remained two-sided, maximum |delta| was 7.51, and cleanup ended flat with zero loop errors, reconnects or self-trades. These figures describe that simulation.
