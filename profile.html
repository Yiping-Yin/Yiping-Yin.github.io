Cross-asset market making & executionProject · Optiver × UNSW, Data & Algorithms in Trading · May – Aug 2026Simulation+36.50 P&L · 1,222 lots · 187 fillsone-hour Optibook session
Link to this project

A market-making system connecting an equity basket, futures, ETF and options through common index units, depth-matched execution and inventory-aware hedging.

Analyzed 1,003 timestamp-matched OB5X futures–ETF order-book observations from a 20-minute Optibook session. Lead-lag was negligible within three seconds; return correlation rose from 0.032 at one second to 0.522 at 120 seconds, consistent with an Epps-effect pattern.

Normalized the five-stock basket, three futures and ETF into common index units. The system executed depth-matched ETF–futures IOC pairs and quoted two Black-76 options with inventory-aware skew and threshold-based delta hedging.

A one-hour Optibook simulation produced +36.50 P&L change on 1,222 lots and 187 fills. Of the 187 fills, 39 arbitrage pairs matched 892 lots. All 6,972 quote cycles remained two-sided, maximum |delta| was 7.51, and cleanup ended flat with zero loop errors, reconnects or self-trades.

Order-book analytics & ETF pricingProject · Feb – May 2025Diagnostic4.30 bps MAE · 0.999 correlationNDQ fair value / iNAV, 4,259 post-calibration rows
Link to this project

Reconstructing limit-order books and modelling ETF fair value, with strict timestamp alignment and explicit assumptions in historical market-making diagnostics.

Reconciled 4,406 PXA trades to 24,303 eligible L1–L8 order-book states using same-venue, strict-prior matching. Benchmarked four mid-price estimators and produced 34,044 rolling-100 diagnostics with zero look-ahead rows.

The five-second NDQ fair-value/iNAV model used 4,319 synchronized ETF, NQ futures and AUD/USD observations; the beta-1 specification recorded its 4.30 bps MAE and 0.999 correlation on 4,259 post-calibration rows.

A 64-session chronological minute-bar-proxy market-making study included a 12-session historical diagnostic partition. Under identical proxy assumptions, inventory and imbalance controls reduced fills from 1,863 to 1,014 and maximum inventory from 500 to 336; diagnostic net P&L rose from 56.53 to 147.21.

Explaining Markets — earnings-reaction forecastingResearch competition · Chicago Booth × Optiver · Aug 2026 – presentModel50,000 features · 6,147 examplesfrozen TF-IDF/Ridge residual model, Q4 2025 – Q2 2026
Link to this project

A frozen text-based residual model and a signed submission path for earnings-event forecasts, with a separate three-agent challenger designed for shadow evaluation.

Built and froze a 50,000-feature TF-IDF/Ridge residual model on 6,147 asset-event examples from Q4 2025 to Q2 2026. Used cutoff-bounded official disclosures, quarter-wise earnings-surprise residualization and a fixed forward scheme, with 2,000 source-event block bootstraps under the pinned official delta R-squared scorer.

Engineered a five-minute signed ACK-first submission path with atomic deduplication, asynchronous inference after acknowledgement and exact-neutral fallbacks.

Designed an E1-anchored three-agent challenger with direction counterfactuals and effective-sample, out-of-distribution and evidence gates for selection-adjusted E2–E5 shadow evaluation.

A reproducible foundation for studying equities, cryptocurrencies and news on their native trading calendars, with explicit data-cleaning and publication-time controls.

The 2020–2023 foundation spans 50 US equities, 10 cryptocurrencies and 149,683 raw headlines. Native-calendar returns and news alignment to the same or next equity trading day preserve market-specific timing.

Removed 2,847 exact duplicates and excluded 10 post-sample crypto rows. Built breadth and regime diagnostics with point-in-time availability and lagged-sentiment controls.
